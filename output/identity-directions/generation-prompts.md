# clear-cut

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished brand direction stylescape for Blur Design, a general design studio that believes clarity first. Title: "01 / CLEAR CUT". Reference image is the EXISTING Blur blackletter monogram and wordmark, not a redesign target. Use its black wordmark once at small navigation scale, preserve its sharp terminals and diamond punctuation as closely as possible. Do not present new logo options.
Landscape composition, 1800x1200 feel, flat front-on design presentation, three purposeful zones: top 60% a realistic website hero; bottom left 45% width typography specimen; bottom right 55% a service content section. White #FAFAF8 with ink #151515, grey #555555, rare cobalt #2448D8 only on one small CTA and a tiny section marker. Extremely precise typography, generous whitespace and strong asymmetric baseline alignment. Hero left reads "Good design.\nClear reasons." in large Manrope Medium-like geometric sans, right has compact text "Brand identity, product UI and design elevation. Every decision has a reason." Top navigation Work / Approach / Services / Contact. A small blackletter B at far left and generous breathing room. Bottom edge hero a hairline rule and selected-work caption. Type specimen "Manrope / DM Sans" with "Aa 0123456789" and medium-size phrase "Clarity first." DM Sans-like body sample beneath. Service zone reads "01 Brand identity", "02 Product UI", "03 Design elevation", each on its own thin rule with one compact description. Small diamond is the only expressive graphic detail derived from reference. Composition is about contrast between expressive historic logo and calm contemporary type. No huge blackletter headlines, no chrome, no gradients, no 3D objects, no fake projects, no rounded SaaS cards, no cream/olive styling, no typography collage, no mockup laptop. Keep logo subordinate to proposition. Clearly legible, well-spaced professional identity presentation.

# the-rationale

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one polished brand direction stylescape for Blur Design, general design studio, concept "02 / THE RATIONALE": clarity comes from making the thinking visible. Reference image shows the existing Blur blackletter B and Blur wordmark. Use existing mark as an editorial signature once, preserve silhouette; do NOT invent logo alternatives.
Landscape design presentation ~1800x1200. Three clear zones: website hero top half, type specimen lower-left, editorial process section lower-right. Cool paper #F3F4F0, charcoal #202420, dark green #344D3C, pale citron #E6E878 used on ONE small annotation tab occupying <3% of image. Visually rooted in typography foundry specimens, publishing and reasoned design practice. Distinct from a generic cream-and-serif luxury agency: cool white paper, hard column rules, robust legible headings, small chapter labels, almost no rounded elements. Hero top small blackletter Blur wordmark left, navigation Work / Thinking / Services / Contact. Main headline in upright Newsreader Medium-like serif: "Make the\nreason visible." Whole headline same serif face, no decorative italic word swap. To its right, narrow DM Sans-like paragraph "We make identities and interfaces easier to understand — and explain why they work." Content has an intentional annotation gutter and tiny diamond chapter marker borrowed from logo. Lower-left type specimen says "Newsreader / DM Sans", shows "Aa 0123456789" in serif and a plain body sample. Lower-right section with heading "From question to decision." followed by three numbered editorial rows: "01 Understand", "02 Explore", "03 Explain", each with a one-line note and thin grid rules. Add small pale citron marginal note "Why this works". Logo heritage balanced by modern sans body and analytical grid; no parchment, vintage crest, ornamental blackletter body, stock photography, floating 3D, crypto graphics, gradients, dashboards, or generic feature cards. Refined but purposeful, generous white space, persuasive information hierarchy.

# measured-impact

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one brand direction stylescape for Blur Design, a GENERAL design studio, concept "03 / MEASURED IMPACT": remove the noise, reveal what matters. The supplied image is their existing blackletter B monogram and Blur wordmark, identity reference not a redesign. Use white wordmark small in top navigation, preserve recognizable silhouette and diamond ending. No invented logo option.
Landscape professional stylescape ~1800x1200, top 60% realistic website hero, bottom left typography specimen, bottom right one story content section. Dominantly deep charcoal #171918 with chalk #F4F4F0, muted light grey #B5B9B4, apricot #F3A96B for small CTA and chapter marker (<6% area). Archivo Bold-like sans display, confident but controlled, headline upper/lowercase "Less noise.\nMore meaning." on left. Narrow right intro "Brand identity, product UI and design elevation. Built around a clear point of view." Layout has unusually wide intervals, a cinematic rectangular media window beneath headline showing an abstract close-up of matte white cut-paper curves and one sharply defined diamond-shaped cutout inspired by logo negative space; tactile paper, light and shade, completely unbranded conceptual artwork, not fake client evidence. No glowing orbs, chrome or NFT art. Media represents still of reveal motion, not interface. Top nav Work / Approach / Services / Contact in restrained IBM Plex Sans-like type. Bottom-left type specimen "Archivo / IBM Plex Sans", "Aa 0123456789", and "Clarity first." Bottom-right section heading "A clear point of view." with three horizontally ruled rows "Identity", "Product", "Elevation" and compact text about choices and rationale. The logo is the expressive signature, rest is functional typography. No massive gothic headline, cyberpunk, terminal UI, lime/purple neon, gradients, bento cards, spaceship or decorative serif swaps. Calm first glance with strong cinematic pacing. Highly legible, sharp corners, few elements.
